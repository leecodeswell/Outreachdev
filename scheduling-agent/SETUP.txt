SCHEDULING AGENT: CONNECTING SIGN-IN AND TEAM ACCESS
====================================================

Files in this folder
  index.html, app.css, app.js, solver.js, favicon.svg   the app
  cloud.js         talks to Firebase (sign-in + shared data)
  config.js        YOUR Firebase settings go here
  firestore.rules  the security rules (who can see and change what)

1. Paste your settings
   Open config.js. Replace   window.SA_FIREBASE_CONFIG = null;
   with the firebaseConfig block from Firebase, like:
   window.SA_FIREBASE_CONFIG = { apiKey: "...", authDomain: "...", projectId: "...", ... };

2. Turn on the security rules (important)
   Firebase console > Build > Firestore Database > Rules tab.
   Delete what's there, paste the whole contents of firestore.rules, press Publish.

3. Put the folder online
   Copy this whole folder into your website repo as "scheduling-agent", commit and push.
   It will be at https://outreachdev.dev/scheduling-agent/
   (To test on your computer first: run  py -m http.server 8000  in this folder
    and open http://localhost:8000 . localhost is already allowed in Firebase.)

4. First sign-in
   Open the app, create your account (or use Google), then "Create my workspace".
   If you set things up earlier in this same browser, tick "Bring in what's saved in this browser".

5. Invite your team
   Team page > Invite next to a person > send them the link.
   They sign in with Google or email and land on their own phone view.

Without step 1 the app still works, saving only in your browser like before.

PAYWALL: FREE TRIAL, THEN PAID  (new in this version)
=====================================================
- Every new business gets a 14-day free trial, then the owner is locked out of EDITING until they pay.
  Their data stays safe and employees can still see published schedules.
- Prices and plan names are in config.js (SA_BILLING). Change them any time.
- You MUST publish the new firestore.rules again (Firestore > Rules > paste > Publish). The lock is enforced there.
- Your existing test workspace was made before this, so it stays open. To test the paywall, sign up with a
  different email/Google account.

Unlocking a customer by hand (works with no payment provider at all)
  Firebase console > Firestore Database > Data > businesses > (the business id) >
  Add collection "billing" > document id "plan" > fields:
      paid  (boolean)  true
      plan  (string)   lifetime      (or "monthly")
      ai    (boolean)  false
  The app unlocks within a second. Set paid to false to lock again.
  Finding the business id: the "users" collection, the document for their sign-in, field "bid".

Automatic unlocking after payment: see scheduling-agent-backend/BACKEND-SETUP.txt (Lemon Squeezy section).
