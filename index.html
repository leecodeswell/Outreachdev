Why not just buy leads?

Marketplaces sell the same homeowner to four of your competitors.

Angi, Thumbtack and the rest make money per lead, so the same request goes to several pros at once, and whoever calls first wins the race to the bottom. We do the opposite: every market has one builder, one roofer, one remodeler, and every lead goes to them alone.

Shared-lead marketplaces

  • One request sold to several contractors at the same time
  • Homeowners shopping on price, not on the quality of your work
  • You pay for leads that never answer the phone
  • The platform owns the reviews, the profile and the relationship

Working with us

  • One contractor per trade, per market. Your leads are yours.
  • Homeowners who found you, saw your work, and asked for you
  • Every lead qualified by a person before it reaches you
  • You own the website, the Google profile, the reviews and the data

Common questions

Straight answers before you book a call.

The full list is on the FAQ page. These are the four we hear first.

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. We sign one contractor per trade in each market, and every lead we generate in that market goes to that contractor only. We don't resell, recycle or split leads.

How do you charge?

A flat monthly fee for the scope we agree on, in writing, before work starts. No setup fees, no per-lead surprises and no long-term contract. We'll walk through the exact number on the call.

How fast do leads start?

If you already have a decent website and Google profile, outreach and marketing can start within the first couple of weeks. If we're building your presence from scratch, expect a build month first, then a ramp. We'll give you an honest timeline for your market.

What if I stop? Do I lose everything?

No. The website, domain, Google Business Profile, reviews and content are yours. We hand over every login. That's the opposite of a marketplace profile.
