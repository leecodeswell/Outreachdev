About

I'm Lee. I build the system, run it, and pick up when you call.

Pari is a one-person operation on purpose. You get an engineer who writes the code, studies your market, qualifies your leads and is accountable for the result, instead of an account manager reading from a dashboard.

Background

Engineering student at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University. Before Pari, I started Ares Web Services, where I design and code websites from scratch for small businesses and build the software behind them.

What I've built

A scheduling app that builds fair shift schedules for part-time teams, a work-order manager that machine shops run on the floor with printed QR codes, quote builders, and a lot of fast, hand-coded websites. All of it live at ares-web.com.

Why contractors

I kept meeting contractors who did outstanding work and still lost jobs to louder competitors with worse craftsmanship and better Google results. That's a fixable problem, and it's the one I decided to spend my time on.

How I work

Five commitments, in writing, on every engagement.

One contractor per trade, per market

I don't take your competitor. If your market is taken, I'll say so on the first call.

Scope and price before work starts

A one-page agreement with a flat monthly number. No per-lead bidding and no credits.

You own everything

Domain, site, Google Business Profile, content, data, logins. If we stop, it all stays with you.

Month to month

No annual contract. I'd rather earn next month than lock you into it.

Honest marketing, only

No fake reviews, no spam, no bought lists, no pretending to be someone we're not. Everything we do for you is something you'd be comfortable explaining to a customer.

A real person, every time

Call or text 641-596-0111. If I'm on another call, you'll hear back the same day.

Want to talk it through?

Twenty minutes, no pitch deck. Bring your questions.
