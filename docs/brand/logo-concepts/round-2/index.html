Pari logo concepts, round 2

Starting fresh: no pear, just a professional lead gen agency. Every mark is flat, two colors, built from straight lines and plain arcs, with a P or an idea from the work itself: reach, filtering, verification, targeting.

01

Signal

A P whose bowl is two arcs, like a signal going out. Reads as reach and finding people.

Pari
02

Lead to client

One line from a starting point to an end point, drawn as a P. A lead found, a client won.

Pari
03

Filter

Three bars narrowing to one orange point: many businesses in, one verified lead out.

Pari
04

Stencil P

A P cut into two plain pieces with a gap, like a stencil. Simple, sturdy, reads at any size.

Pari
05

Verified P

A solid P with a check mark cut through the bowl. Says "verified" without a word.

Pari
06

Flag P

The bowl of the P is a pennant flag on a pole: a marker planted on the next customer.

Pari
07

Viewfinder

Four corner marks framing one orange point, like a camera locking on. Targeted, not spray-and-pray.

Pari
08

Wordmark

No symbol: "pari" in a plain geometric sans, with a square orange full stop. The icon is "p.".
