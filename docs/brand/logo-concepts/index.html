Pari logo concepts

P + pear + pair. Drawn only from circles, straight lines and one leaf shape, two colors, no gradients or gloss. The wordmark is set in Outfit, a plain geometric sans.

01

Pear P, line

The P's stem is the flat side of the pear, carried down. One stroke weight, square top corner so it reads as a P.

Pari
02

Pear P, solid

The same idea filled in, with a round counter so it still reads as a P at favicon size.

Pari
03

The pair

The pear P with a partner pear tucked in beside it. A P, a pear and a pair in one mark.

Pari
04

Two halves

A pear cut down the middle. The orange half and the stem make the p; the dark half is its match.

Pari
05

Two circles

A pear is literally a pair of circles. Where they overlap is cut out, the way a lead and a client match.
