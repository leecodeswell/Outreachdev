Pari researches and verifies leads for the Client. The Client contacts them and makes the sale. The Client pays nothing for the first three customers won from Pari's leads, and in return gives an honest testimonial and lets Pari check results. This page sets out how that works.

01What Pari delivers

  • Researches businesses that match the Ideal Customer in Section 2 and names the decision maker at each one.
  • Checks every lead before delivery: the business is active and in the service area, the decision maker is current, the email is verified and the phone number is checked.
  • Delivers leads in writing, by email or on a shared Notion page, and records the date each lead is delivered.

Pari does not contact leads, quote, sell or sign anything for the Client. The Client handles all contact, estimates, sales and the work. If the Client wants Pari to run outreach or sales, that is a separate monthly retainer agreement.

03Definitions

Delivered Lead: a business or decision maker Pari delivers to the Client in writing. If the Client already had them as a customer, or was quoting or in talks with them in the past 12 months, the Client has 3 business days after delivery to say so in writing. Pari then replaces that lead and it does not count.

Won Client: a Delivered Lead that signs a contract with the Client, or pays a deposit or first payment, within 12 months of delivery. This includes work signed through a different contact or a related company at the same business.

04The founding offer and fees

Won Clients 1–3No charge Each one after$ per Won Client

Pari sends a monthly statement of Won Clients. A fee is due within 15 days after the Client receives the first payment from a Won Client. If a job is cancelled and the deposit is fully refunded before work starts, no fee is owed.

05Lead quality

If a Delivered Lead fails the checks in Section 1, for example the email bounces, the person has left or the business is outside the service area, the Client tells Pari within 14 days of delivery and Pari replaces it free. While this agreement runs, Pari gives each lead to one client only in the Client's trade and service area, and never resells it.

06The Client agrees to

  • Contact Delivered Leads lawfully, following the CAN-SPAM Act, the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, Do Not Call rules and state law, and honor every opt-out.
  • Use Delivered Leads only for its own business, and not share or resell them.
  • Verification: during this agreement and for 12 months after it ends, give Pari read-only access to its CRM or job-tracking system, limited to Delivered Leads. Without one, the Client sends copies of contracts or invoices for Delivered Leads within 5 business days of Pari asking. Pari may also ask a Delivered Lead whether their job went ahead.
  • After the third Won Client, or at the end of the founding period, give an honest written testimonial. Pari may name the Client and share real results. The Client reviews anything before it is published, and Pari will disclose that the Client received services at no cost.

07Term and ending

The founding period lasts 90 days or until 3 Won Clients, whichever comes first. After that, the agreement continues at the per-client fee above unless either party ends it. Either party may end it at any time with 7 days' written notice, and email counts. Fees remain owed for any Delivered Lead that becomes a Won Client within 12 months of delivery, even after this agreement ends.

08Results and confidentiality

Pari does not guarantee a number of Won Clients or sales. Pari's research methods stay Pari's. Each party keeps the other's pricing, customer and business information confidential.

09General

Pari is an independent business, not the Client's employee or agent, and neither party can bind the other. The Client is responsible for its own outreach, work, licenses, insurance and warranties. Neither party is liable to the other for indirect or lost-profit damages. Iowa law governs. This page is the whole agreement, and changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
