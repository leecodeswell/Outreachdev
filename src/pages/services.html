01 · Exclusive lead generation

Qualified homeowner projects, delivered to you and nobody else.

This is the core of what we do. We find homeowners in your service area who are planning the kind of project you want more of, qualify them by phone or message, and hand the conversation to you.

How leads are generated

Local search and your Google Business Profile, your website, social content showing real projects, and direct outreach to homeowners and referral partners in your area. No purchased lists, no recycled marketplace leads.

How leads are qualified

A real person confirms the project scope, timeline, location and that the homeowner expects your call. If it doesn't meet the bar we agreed on, you never see it and you never pay for it.

How leads are delivered

Text and email the moment they're qualified, with name, number, address, project notes and the best time to call. Weekly summary so you can see what's working.

What exclusive means

One contractor per trade in each market. We don't sign your competitor, and we don't send the same lead to two people. Ever.

02 · Websites & local SEO

A website that shows your best work and ranks where homeowners look.

No templates and no page-builder subscriptions. Every site is designed and coded from scratch around your projects, loads fast on a phone, and is built to turn a visit into a call or a form.

  • Project galleries and service pages written for the searches homeowners actually make
  • Google Business Profile setup or cleanup, categories, photos, posts and Q&A
  • Local SEO: citations, service-area pages, schema and reviews that show up in Maps
  • Visibility in AI answers: clear, factual pages that search engines and AI assistants can cite
  • Analytics and call tracking so you know which lead came from where

You own the domain, the site, the hosting account and every login. If we part ways, it all stays with you.
