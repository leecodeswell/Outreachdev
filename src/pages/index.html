What makes a lead "verified"?

A person has confirmed the business is active, identified the right decision maker, checked that the email address accepts mail and the phone number reaches the business, and written down why they fit. If any of that can't be confirmed, the lead isn't delivered.

How much does it cost?

On our main plan you only pay for verified clients: you pay when a lead we delivered becomes your paying client, and nothing for the ones that don't. Other payment plans are available if you'd prefer, and we'll go through them on the first call.

Do I need both lead generation and marketing?

No. Plenty of businesses only want verified leads for their own sales team. Others only want marketing. They work best together, but you choose.

Who does the work?

Lee Thompson, directly. You talk to the person doing the research, building the site and running the campaigns.
