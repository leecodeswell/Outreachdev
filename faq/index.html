FAQ

Straight answers to the questions contractors ask first.

If yours isn't here, ask it on the call or text 641-596-0111.

Exclusivity & leads

Are the leads really exclusive?

Yes. We sign one contractor per trade in each market, and every lead we generate in that market goes to that contractor only. We don't resell, recycle or split leads. If a competitor already holds your market, we'll tell you on the first call.

How is a lead qualified?

A real person confirms the project, the location, the timeline and a realistic budget range, and makes sure the homeowner expects your call. If it doesn't meet the criteria we agreed on, you never see it.

How are leads delivered?

By text and email the moment they're qualified, with name, number, address, project notes and the best time to call. You also get a weekly summary of what came in and where it came from.

How fast do leads start?

If your website and Google Business Profile are already solid, marketing and outreach can start within the first two weeks. If we're building your presence from scratch, plan on about a month of build before leads ramp. You'll get an honest timeline for your market, not a generic promise.

How is this different from Angi, HomeAdvisor or Thumbtack?

Marketplaces sell the same request to several contractors and keep the profile, the reviews and the relationship. We work with one contractor per market, qualify leads by hand, and build everything in your name so you own it.

Do you use spam or purchased lead lists?

No. No bought lists, no bulk unsolicited email, no fake reviews, no robocalls. Outreach is done by a person, follows federal and state telemarketing and email rules, and is something you'd be comfortable explaining to a customer.

Pricing, contracts & logistics

How do you charge?

A flat monthly fee for the scope we agree on, in writing, before work starts. No setup fees, no per-lead bidding, no credits. The exact number depends on your trade and market, and you'll get it on the call, not from a tier chart.

Is there a contract?

A one-page agreement that covers scope and price, month to month. No annual lock-in.

What happens if I stop?

You keep everything: website, domain, hosting, Google Business Profile, reviews, content and every login. That's the opposite of a marketplace profile, which disappears the day you stop paying.

What areas do you serve?

Based in Clear Lake, Iowa, working across Iowa, Minnesota, Nebraska and the Midwest. The work is remote, so contractors anywhere in the United States are welcome.

Which trades do you work with?

Custom home builders, remodelers and design-build firms, roofing and exteriors, HVAC, plumbing and electrical, and landscape, hardscape, concrete and outdoor living. Other high-ticket local trades case by case. See who we serve.

Who will I actually be working with?

Lee Thompson, directly. Lee builds the site, runs the marketing, qualifies the leads and answers the phone. More about Lee.
