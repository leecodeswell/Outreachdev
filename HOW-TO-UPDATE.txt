Outreachdev website, version 16
================================

WHAT'S IN HERE
  Everything in this folder goes in the TOP LEVEL of your website repo, the same
  place index.html already is. It includes both apps:
    scheduling-agent/   Scheduling Agent (adds a demo mode: .../scheduling-agent/?demo)
    work-orders/        Work Order Manager (adds a demo mode: .../work-orders/?demo)

HOW TO UPDATE (GitHub website)
  1. Open your repo, click "Add file" > "Upload files".
  2. Drag in EVERYTHING inside this folder (the files and the folders), not the
     outer folder itself.
  3. Commit straight to main. GitHub replaces the old files with these.
  4. Delete scheduler.js from the repo (open it, click the trash icon, commit).
     The old demo used it; nothing needs it now.

  No Firebase changes this time. Your rules and backend stay exactly as they are;
  the demos run entirely in the visitor's browser and never touch your database.

WHAT CHANGED
  - Home page: new hero animation, Websites & Apps and AI Chatbots sections moved
    up front, Applications shows the two live apps with demos and free trials,
    "Under development" is a closed drop-down with three upcoming apps.
  - New page: work-order-manager.html. scheduling-agent.html is rewritten.
  - spreadsheet-to-app.html, quote-builder.html and work-instructions.html now just
    forward visitors to the right place, so old links keep working.
  - The contact form has an "Interested in" choice, and "Ask about it" buttons
    fill it in for the visitor.

DEMO MODE
  Adding ?demo to either app's address opens a copy with sample data, no sign-in.
  It's saved in that browser tab only and erased when the tab closes, so it can't
  be used as a free version of the app. A small bar links to the free trial.
