What a verified lead includes

Six things every lead has before it reaches you.

If any of them can't be confirmed, the lead isn't delivered. You never pay for a guess.

Illustrative example. Real records carry full names and direct contact details.

  1. A real, active businessConfirmed open and operating, with a working website or listing, not a dead entry from an old database.
  2. The right personThe owner, manager or buyer who actually decides, by name and role.
  3. A verified emailChecked to accept mail before delivery, so your outreach doesn't bounce and hurt your sending reputation.
  4. A checked phone numberA number confirmed to reach the business, marked as direct line or main office.
  5. Why they fitSize, location, services and the signal that makes them worth a call this month.
  6. Where it came fromThe public sources used, so you can trust it and so it can be checked again later.

How the research works

Four steps, done by a person.

  1. Define your ideal customerIndustry, size, location, and the signals that mean someone is ready to buy. We write it down together so the target is clear.
  2. ResearchWe search company sites, directories, public registries, job boards, permits and social profiles for businesses and people that match.
  3. VerifyWe confirm the business is active, find the decision maker, check the email and phone, and note why they fit. Anything we can't confirm is dropped.
  4. DeliverEvery week, in a shared spreadsheet or straight into your CRM, with a short summary of what came in.

Pricing

Pay only for verified clients.

Our main plan is simple. You pay when a lead we delivered becomes your paying client, and nothing for the leads that don't. Every lead we send is tracked in a shared sheet, so both of us can see what closed.
